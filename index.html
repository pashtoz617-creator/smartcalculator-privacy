Smart Calculator – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Smart Calculator (package name com.baratdarman.smartcalculator), developed by Muhammad Barat Darman ("we", "us"), handles information. The app offers a calculator, unit converter, age calculator, discount calculator and a Gregorian / Shamsi / Hijri calendar converter, in Pashto, Dari and English.

Information you enter

Numbers, dates and other values you type into the tools are processed only on your device. They are not sent to us and we do not store them on any server. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information collected by third-party services

The app uses two Google services. They may collect the information below automatically when you use the app.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) – shows advertisements. It may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system version, and how you interact with ads. It uses this to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) – helps us understand how the app is used. It may collect an app-instance identifier, device and operating system information, approximate location (country) derived from your IP address, and simple usage events such as which tool screen you opened and which language you selected.

Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Privacy and security in Firebase
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ads personalization in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only your preferences (selected language and light/dark theme) in the app's private storage on your phone.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with Google through the services described above.

Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal profiles. Analytics and advertising data held by Google is kept according to Google's own policies and settings. To ask about data related to your device, contact us at the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date above will change.
